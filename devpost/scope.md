# Lead Rescue AI

A lightweight AI assistant for small service businesses to spot cold leads and generate personalized follow-up messages in seconds.

## The Unique Kernel
Explainable lead triage paired with instant, context-aware message generation. Rather than a static spreadsheet or an overwhelming enterprise CRM, the app explains *why* a prospect is slipping away and generates an empathetic revival message tailored to their specific enquiry and inaction duration. If an API key or network connection is unavailable, it gracefully falls back to deterministic, high-quality templates while transparently labeling the generation source.

## Who It's For
Small service-business owners and operators (such as plumbers, HVAC technicians, electricians, and independent contractors) who receive customer enquiries across WhatsApp, web forms, social channels, and phone calls. 
- **Current reality:** Prospects ask for quotes or availability and go silent; operators are too busy on job sites to track every conversation or spend 15 minutes drafting personalized follow-ups, resulting in lost sales.
- **Goal:** Quickly triage overlooked leads and recover high-intent customers with minimal effort.

## The Core Loop
1. **View & Triage:** The operator opens the app and sees incoming/stored leads (including preloaded sample leads or newly entered ones) grouped or filtered by urgency ("Leads Needing Follow-up" and "High-Priority Leads").
2. **Diagnose:** Lead Rescue AI flags leads requiring attention with an explicit reason (e.g., "Received quote 4 days ago with no response", "Enquired about emergency drain repair 48 hours ago").
3. **Generate:** The operator selects a cold lead, picks a follow-up tone (Friendly Check-in, Gentle Nudge, or Value-Focused Follow-up), and generates a tailored follow-up message (via Gemini AI, or via robust fallback with a clear source indicator).
4. **Edit & Copy:** The operator reviews/tweaks the message and clicks a single button to copy it directly into WhatsApp, SMS, or email.

## Inspiration & Identity
- **Tone & Mood:** Fast, clean, purposeful, and business-focused. Zero fluff or complicated setup.
- **Interface Feel:** A clear, modern dashboard that gives immediate visibility into leads needing follow-up and high-priority leads, making follow-up as easy as a single click.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
To demonstrate practical, high-value AI for real-world small business workflows, and to execute a disciplined, spec-driven engineering process where scope, requirements, and architecture are validated before writing application code.

## What "Working" Looks Like
In a 60-second walkthrough:
1. The app loads with realistic home-service leads (e.g., pipe replacement quote pending 5 days, water heater inquiry inactive 48 hours).
2. The user adds a new lead or clicks an existing cold lead.
3. The app highlights why this lead is at risk and marks it under "Leads Needing Follow-up" or "High-Priority Leads".
4. The user selects a tone (default: "Friendly Check-in"), clicks "Generate Follow-up", and a tailored follow-up message appears factoring in the customer's name, requested service, and last interaction—clearly showing whether it was AI-generated or generated via template fallback.
5. The user edits a word and clicks "Copy to Clipboard" with immediate visual confirmation.

## The POC Boundary
- **In Scope:**
  - Lead model with essential fields: customer name, service requested, status, last contact date/time, and interaction notes.
  - Preloaded realistic sample leads (plumbing & home services).
  - Manual lead entry form for testing fresh leads.
  - Follow-up need detection with human-readable rationale (categorized as "Leads Needing Follow-up" and "High-Priority Leads").
  - Tone-selectable follow-up message generator (Friendly Check-in [default], Gentle Nudge, Value-Focused).
  - Dual-mode message generation: Gemini API integration with an automatic, reliable offline fallback, clearly labeled in the UI (e.g., "Generated by AI" vs. "Generated by Template Fallback").
  - In-place message editing and one-click copy to clipboard.
  - Simple, responsive web UI with clean visual cues.

## Later
- Custom business profile settings (custom business name, phone number, operating hours).
- Tracking lead status transition to "Followed Up" or "Won/Lost" after copying.
- Exporting leads to CSV or spreadsheet.

## Explicitly Cut
- **Live WhatsApp/SMS API integration:** Adds billing, webhooks, and phone verification complexity that distracts from the core AI workflow.
- **Automated message sending:** Operators must retain review and control over customer communications.
- **User authentication & multi-tenant accounts:** Unnecessary overhead for a focused proof-of-concept hackathon demo.
- **Complex CRM pipelines, artificial revenue metrics, and analytics:** Focus is strictly on lead rescue triage and message drafting without speculative financial numbers.
